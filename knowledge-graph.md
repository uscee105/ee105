# Knowledge Graph
{:.no_toc}

An interactive map of **185 concepts** and **278 relationships** across EE 105 — how
every idea in the course connects to the ones before and after it. Use it to review
for exams, to see why a topic is introduced when it is, or to trace a concept back to
its prerequisites.

[Open the Knowledge Graph]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/knowledge-graph/){: .btn .btn-primary .fs-5 .mb-4 .mb-md-0 .mr-2 target="_blank" }

---

## Access

The graph is **password protected** and available to enrolled EE 105 students only.
The class password is posted on
[Brightspace](https://brightspace.usc.edu/d2l/home/263211).

You will be asked for it the first time you open the page. Tick
**"Keep me signed in on this device"** and you will not be prompted again for 30 days —
useful if you are scanning the QR codes on lecture slides during class.

## What's inside

| Unit | Coverage |
|:-----|:---------|
| Circuit Theory | Sections 1–13 |
| Devices & Semiconductors | Diodes, transistors, junctions |
| Optoelectronics | Emitters, detectors, photonics |
| Sensors | Sections 27–30 |
| Op-Amps & Filters | Sections 36–37 |
| Linear Algebra | Section 42 |
| Machine Learning | Section 43 |

Each node carries a plain-language definition, and each edge is labelled with the
relationship it represents — *"current measures flow of charge"*, and so on. Click any
node to see its definition, its prerequisites, and what it leads to. Use the search box
to jump to a concept, or the legend chips to filter the view down to a single unit.
