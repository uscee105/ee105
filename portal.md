# Course portal

Log in with your student ID and portal PIN to submit your lab results for **Lab 4.2 (Camera
classifier)** and **Lab 5.1 (LiFi)**. Each lab is submitted as one file, the
`lab<N.k>_submission.json` that the last cell of the lab notebook writes; it is graded when you
upload it.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

<div id="quiz-app"><noscript>The course portal needs JavaScript.</noscript><p>Loading the course portal...</p></div>

**Labs.** Run the lab notebook to the end with your board connected, then upload the submission
file it saved in the lab folder. You see your score and the feedback right away. You can upload
again, up to the number of uploads shown for the lab; your best score counts. A file made in practice mode on the simulator
is accepted but scores zero for labs that need the board.
{: .fs-3 }
